HIGH-FIDELITY PHASE AURA DESIGN SYSTEM

SCR-01: Home & Curated Discovery Feed

Vibrant consumer discovery hub featuring an editable location selector, prominent search, category shortcuts, personalized recommendations, and high-impact 2:3 vertical poster cards with upfront "All-in" pricing.

Key UX & Ergonomic Decisions

  • Editable Location: Instant modal allows switching between Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, etc.
  • Radical Transparency: Upfront starting price with "All-in" tag prevents deceptive base-price anchors.
  • Thumb Ergonomics: 4-pillar persistent navigation with active pill indicators and badge counts.
